## Blockers

### B1 - Reduced text-scramble does not show the final text at its exact onset

- violatedCriterion: T13-SCRAMBLE-REDUCED. This identifier names todo 13's explicit requirement, `reduced -> full text at onset`. The shipped card repeats that the final text appears at onset while the source remains before onset.
- evidencePointer: `54fd26f:skills/eidos-motion/techniques/text-scramble/technique.js:101-104`, `54fd26f:skills/eidos-motion/techniques/text-scramble/card.md:75-76`, and the reproduced SHIPPED_ONSET trace below.
- Severity: medium; a deterministic boundary error, not a flaky rendering result.
- Observation: The shipped template's first typewriter begins at t=0.4. Under reduced motion, its visible span is empty at exactly t=0.4, even though its parent row is visible. `hello` exists only in a hidden reserve span. It appears at t=0.400001. A viewer parked at the onset sees an empty value indefinitely.
- Cause: `E.reduced ? (x > 0 ? 1 : 0)` requires positive progress, not the onset. The common `snap` also maps very small positive progress back to zero.
- Correction needed: distinguish a future track from a track at its start, then reveal the full target at the start. Merely changing the comparison to `x >= 0` would reveal future tracks too: `Timeline.seek` invokes future tracks with zero progress. Preserve source-before-start, full-at-start, and seek determinism together.
